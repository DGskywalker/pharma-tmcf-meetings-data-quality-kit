## Part 1: Recreating or Customizing in Canva

### Step 1: Project Creation
1. Log in to [Canva.com](https://canva.com).
2. Click **Create a Design** -> Select **Video (1920 x 1080 px)**.
3. Name your project: `Meridian BioPharma - 60s Meetings Data Quality Explainer`.

### Step 2: Page Setup & Timing
Set up 6 distinct pages on the Canva timeline, each set to exactly **10.0 seconds**:
- **Page 1 (0:00 - 0:10):** *The Regulatory Stakes (Sunshine Act & EFPIA)*
- **Page 2 (0:10 - 0:20):** *The 6 Swimlanes (Architecture)*
- **Page 3 (0:20 - 0:30):** *Where Data Leaks (DLP-1, DLP-2, DLP-4)*
- **Page 4 (0:30 - 0:40):** *The 14-Day Roster Crisis (The Ghost HCP Hazard)*
- **Page 5 (0:40 - 0:50):** *The Requester 4-Point Golden Rule*
- **Page 6 (0:50 - 1:00):** *The 95% Accuracy Target & PharmaInsights Hub*

### Step 3: Canva Color Palette & Styling
Apply Meridian's corporate palette:
- **Canvas Background:** `#0F172A` (Midnight Navy)
- **Content Cards:** `#1E293B` (Slate Dark) with 1px border `#334155`
- **Primary Highlights:** `#0D9488` (Clinical Teal) & `#06B6D4` (Electric Cyan)
- **Alert Highlights:** `#E11D48` (Regulatory Red)
- **Typography:**
  - Headers: **Montserrat ExtraBold** (All Caps)
  - Body: **Open Sans SemiBold**
  - Subtitles: **Montserrat Bold** with black background box

### Step 4: Canva Animations & Transitions
- **Page Transitions:** Select **Slide** or **Dissolve** (Duration: 0.5s).
- **Text Animations:**
  - Headers: Apply **"Ascend"** or **"Typewriter"** effect.
  - Bullet Points: Apply **"Fade"** (Enter: On click or staggered by 0.8s).
- **Bottom Progress Bar:** Insert a rectangle element at `y = 1070`, height `10px`, fill `#0D9488`. Apply **"Wipe Right"** animation spanning the full 60 seconds.

### Step 5: Canva Audio & Voiceover
1. Go to **Apps** -> **Murf AI** or record internal voiceover reading the script in `storyboard_and_script.md`.
2. Under **Audio**, search: `Minimal Corporate Tech`, `Ambient Documentary Synth`, or `Clean Healthcare Piano`.
3. Set music volume to **14%** so voiceover remains crystal clear.

## Part 2: Recreating or Customizing in CapCut (Desktop or Mobile)

CapCut is ideal for high-speed dynamic keyframing, automated captions, and sound effects.

### Step 1: Project Setup
1. Launch **CapCut Desktop** (or mobile app).
2. Click **New Project** -> Menu -> **Modify Project Settings**:
   - Resolution: **1080p (1920x1080)** or **9:16 (1080x1920)**
   - Frame Rate: **30 FPS** or **60 FPS**
   - Color Space: **Rec. 709**

### Step 2: Media Asset Import
Import the generated visual assets from this repository:
- `assets/process_map_overview.png`
- `assets/roster_aging.png`
- `assets/accuracy_by_bu.png`
- `assets/dlp_breakdown.png`
- `assets/compliance_radar.png`
- `video/video_poster.png`

### Step 3: Text-to-Speech (TTS) Voice Generation
1. In CapCut, click **Text** -> **Add Text**.
2. Paste the Voiceover Script from `video/storyboard_and_script.md` for Scene 1.
3. Click the text box -> Select **Text to Speech** in the right inspector panel:
   - Recommended Voices: **"Serious Male"**, **"Confident Narrative Female"**, or **"British Professional"**.
4. Click **Start Reading**; CapCut will generate the timed voiceover audio track automatically on Track A1.
5. Repeat for Scenes 2 through 6. The total spoken duration will calibrate to exactly 59–60 seconds.

### Step 4: Auto-Captions with Corporate Styling
1. Navigate to **Captions** -> **Auto-Captions** -> Click **Generate**.
2. CapCut will generate synced speech subtitles across all 60 seconds.
3. Select all caption clips -> Inspector -> **Text Template**:
   - Style: Font **Proxima Nova / Montserrat**, Size: **18**, Color: **#FFFFFF**.
   - Background: Solid Black box (`#000000`), Opacity **85%**, Corner radius **4px**.
   - Position: `Y = -380` (Bottom center above the progress bar).

### Step 5: Sound Effects & Impact Cues
Enhance engagement with subtle sound design:
- **Scene Transitions (0:10, 0:20, 0:30, 0:40, 0:50):** Add SFX `Swish 02` or `Whoosh Fast`.
- **DLP Warnings (0:22, 0:25, 0:28):** Add SFX `Digital Error Glitch` or `Subtle Ping`.
- **Golden Rule Checkmarks (0:42, 0:44, 0:46, 0:48):** Add SFX `Pop Bubble` or `Success Ding`.

### Step 6: Export Specifications
- **Format:** `MP4 (H.264 / AAC)`
- **Resolution:** `1080p (1920 x 1080)`
- **Bitrate:** `Higher / 12 Mbps`
- **Audio:** `320 kbps Stereo`
