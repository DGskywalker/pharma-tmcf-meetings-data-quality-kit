## 1. Canvas Setup & Dimensions
- **Canva Doc Type:** "Newsletter" or "A4 / US Letter Document"
- **Dimensions:** 
  - Print Standard (US Letter): `8.5 x 11.0 inches` (or `2550 x 3300 px` at 300 DPI)
  - Screen / PDF Digital Standard: `816 x 1056 px` (72 DPI)
- **Margins & Safe Zones:**
  - Outer Margin: `0.5 inches (36 pt)` on all sides
  - Gutter between main body and sidebar: `0.35 inches (25 pt)`

## 3. Typography Pairings in Canva

### Header & Display Typography
- **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** (Font Weight: Bold 700 / ExtraBold 800)
  - Alternative in Canva: *League Spartan*, *Aileron Heavy*, or *Proxima Nova*
  - Usage: Masthead "THE DATA PULSE" (32 pt), Article titles (16–18 pt), Metric numbers (24 pt)

### Body & Narrative Typography
- **Body Font:** **Open Sans** (Font Weight: Regular 400 / Semi-Bold 600)
  - Alternative in Canva: *Calibri*, *Inter*, or *Source Sans Pro*
  - Usage: Article paragraphs (9.5 pt, Line spacing: 1.45), Quotations (Italic 9 pt)

### Metadata & Badges
- **Tag Font:** **Montserrat** (Font Weight: Bold 700, All Caps, Letter spacing: 1.5)
  - Usage: Category pill badges, table column headers (7.5 pt)

## 5. Canva Search Terms for Graphical Assets
To reproduce the visual flair in Canva's element library:
- **Icons:** Search `Checkmark square flat`, `Warning triangle outline`, `Credit card chip`, `Hospital medical cross`, `Database analytics`
- **Frames:** Search `Rounded rectangle frame 8px`, `Vertical separator line 1px`
- **Charts:** Search `Horizontal gauge`, `Donut chart progress` (Set value to 78% teal, 22% red)
